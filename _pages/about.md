I am a PhD Student at [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), Switzerland working on AI for ultrasound under the supervision of [Prof. Dr. Luca Benini](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8riq3sYAAAAJ) and [Dr. Christoph Leitner](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_gUK_isAAAAJ). My current research focuses on development of novel and efficient AI-based algorithms for wearable ultrasound.

Previously, I did my Master's at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/), Switzerland where I was working on different applications of deep learning including Neural Compression, Multimodal LLMs, Explainable AI, Deepfake Detection, and Lensless Imaging. I did my Master's Thesis at [EPFL MLO](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/mlo/).
Besides, I had been teaching Deep Learning at [HSE](https://www.hse.ru/en/), Russia and at [LauzHack](https://lauzhack.com/), EPFL. I have also worked at [Sony](https://www.sony.com/en/) for 6 months as Research Intern, at [EPFL LCAV](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lcav/) laboratory for 4 months as Research Intern, at [Reality Defender Inc.](https://www.realitydefender.com/) for 9 months as Research Intern, and at [Samsung R&D Institute, Russia](https://research.samsung.com/srr) for 2.5 years as an assistant at the Biometric Solutions Laboratory.
